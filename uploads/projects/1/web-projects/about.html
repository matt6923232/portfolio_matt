About CCTE

The College of Computing and Technology Engineering provides students with strong foundations in computing, engineering technology, innovation, and practical problem-solving.

The department promotes hands-on learning, research, technical skills, teamwork, and global exposure through academic programs and international activities.

Mission & Vision

Mission

To develop competent, responsible, and innovative students equipped with technical knowledge, professional values, and industry-ready skills.

Vision

To become a recognized center of excellence in computing, technology, engineering, innovation, and global academic collaboration.
