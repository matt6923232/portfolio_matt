ABOUT THE PRODUCT

CLEANING
MADE EASIER.

The Assistant Automatic Cleaner is a proposed robotic cleaning device intended to assist users with routine floor cleaning.

The concept features a rounded body equipped with LED indicators, solar-assisted components, wheels, a trash hopper, and a control button.

The robot is designed to work together with a mobile application, allowing users to conveniently operate and control the cleaning device.
