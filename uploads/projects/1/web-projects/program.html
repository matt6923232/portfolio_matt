CCTE Academic Programs

The College of Computing & Technology Engineering (CCTE) prepares students for real-world industry demands through intensive training in software development, systems design, hardware engineering, and emerging technologies. Our programs are designed to produce globally competitive Computer professionals.

Degree Programs

Software & Intelligence

BS Computer Science

A program focused on the theory and application of computing, software systems, and intelligent technologies. Students are trained to design, build, and optimize digital solutions used in modern industries.

  • Software Development (Web & Mobile Apps)
  • Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning Basics
  • Data Structures & Algorithms
  • Database Systems & Cloud Concepts
  • Problem Solving & System Design
Hardware + Software Engineering

BS Computer Engineering

A program that combines electrical engineering and computer science. It focuses on designing computer systems, embedded devices, robotics, and digital hardware integration.

  • Microprocessors & Embedded Systems
  • Digital Logic Design
  • Robotics & Automation Fundamentals
  • Computer Hardware Architecture
  • System Integration & Engineering Design
